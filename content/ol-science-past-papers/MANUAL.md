### 2024(2025) Q10 — Biology › Biological processes in human body
Figure: `figures/2024-q10-q11-digestive-system.png`

Which of the following options indicates the path the food passes through from A to G in this system respectively?

### 2024(2025) Q13 — Chemistry › Changes in matter
Figure: `figures/2024-q13-delivery-tubes.png` (the options are the four drawings)

Which of the following is the correct way of positioning the delivery tubes to collect two samples of the gases carbon
dioxide and hydrogen into gas jars?

Options: (1) (2) (3) **(4)** ✅

Explanation: carbon dioxide is denser than air, so it is collected in an upright jar with the tube reaching the
bottom (upward displacement of air). Hydrogen is lighter than air, so it is collected in an inverted jar with the tube
reaching the top (downward displacement of air).

### 2024(2025) Q28 — Physics › Electronics
Figure: `figures/2024-q28-circuit.png`

When the switch S is closed in this circuit,

1. bulbs A and B light. 2. bulbs B and C light. 3. **bulbs A and C light.** ✅ 4. all the bulbs light.

Explanation: a diode conducts only when forward biased. The diode in series with A is forward biased, the one with B
is reverse biased, and C is in series with a resistor, so A and C light.

### 2024(2025) Q35 — Physics › Turning effect of a force (add this topic)
Figure: `figures/2024-q35-lamina.png`

A light, rectangular lamina 0.4 m × 0.3 m is pivoted at point O and two forces 5 N and 4 N are applied on it as shown
in the diagram. What is the moment of force on the lamina around the point O?

1. 1.6 N m anticlockwise 2. **1.6 N m clockwise** ✅ 3. 1.5 N m anticlockwise 4. 1.5 N m clockwise

Explanation: the 5 N force acts along the 0.4 m side through O, so its moment about O is zero. The 4 N force acts at a
perpendicular distance of 0.4 m: 4 × 0.4 = 1.6 N m, clockwise.

### 2024(2025) Q37 — Chemistry › Rate of reactions
Figure: `figures/2024-q37-graphs.png` (the options are the four graphs)

A sheet of zinc suspended on a spring balance is immersed in a hydrochloride acid solution bringing them to react. Some
time after the beginning of the reaction another quantity of the acid solution was added into the reaction mixture. If
the spring balance reading (m) was plotted against time (t), which of the following graphs is most likely to be
obtained?

Options: (1) (2) (3) **(4)** ✅

Explanation: the mass falls quickly, then more slowly as the acid is used up, levelling off. Adding fresh acid makes
the mass fall quickly again.

### 2025(2026) Q22 — Physics › Hydrostatic pressure and its applications
Figure: `figures/2025-q22-liquids.png`

There are two immiscible liquids A and B whose densities are ρA and ρB respectively. Three solid spheres X, Y and Z
equal in radius and with densities ρX, ρY and ρZ respectively are in the liquids as shown in the diagram. What is the
correct statement about them?

1. ρA < ρB and ρX = ρY < ρZ
2. **ρA < ρB and ρX < ρY < ρZ** ✅
3. ρA = ρX = ρY and ρB < ρZ
4. ρA = ρY < ρX and ρB = ρZ

Explanation: A floats on B, so ρA < ρB. X floats on A (ρX < ρA), Y stays inside A (ρY = ρA), and Z sinks to the
bottom of B (ρZ > ρB), so ρX < ρY < ρZ.

### 2025(2026) Q26 — Physics › Current electricity
Figure: `figures/2025-q26-resistors.png`

Of P, Q, R and S, in which pair of resistor systems is the value of the equivalent resistance the same between X and Y?

1. P and Q 2. **P and S** ✅ 3. Q and R 4. R and S

Explanation: P is two 2 Ω in parallel = 1 Ω. S is two branches of (1 Ω + 1 Ω) = 2 Ω in parallel = 1 Ω. Q is 2/3 Ω
and R is 1 Ω + 2 Ω = 3 Ω.

### 2025(2026) Q36 — Chemistry › Electrochemistry
Figure: `figures/2025-q36-cells.png`

A, B and C are three sets of simple cells. In the external circuits of the cells A, B and C above, an electric current
flows only in,

1. **A.** ✅ 2. B. 3. A and C. 4. A and B.

Explanation: a simple cell needs two different metals in an electrolyte. A has zinc and copper in dilute sulphuric
acid. In B the two electrodes touch each other inside the acid, so electrons pass directly between them instead of
through the external circuit. C uses the same metal (zinc) for both electrodes, so there is no potential difference.
